Cambridge, UK – 26th November 2025 – SPT Labtech, a leader in life science tools and technologies, today announced a major commitment to environmental sustainability, confirming its near-term greenhouse gas (GHG) emission reduction targets have been officially validated by a rigorous, science-based global standard: the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) (sciencebasedtargets.org). This commitment reinforces its dedication to supporting the sustainability objectives of its customers and partners.
