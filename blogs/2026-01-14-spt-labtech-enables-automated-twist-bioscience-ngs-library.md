Cambridge, UK, 14 January 2026 : SPT Labtech, a global leader in the design and development of laboratory automation and liquid handling solutions, today announced the availability of validated automated workflows for Twist Bioscience’s next-generation sequencing (NGS) library preparation kits on its firefly ® liquid handling platform. Developed through a collaboration with Twist Bioscience , the workflows are designed to support higher-throughput, more reproducible NGS library preparation and are accessible directly through the firefly cloud.
