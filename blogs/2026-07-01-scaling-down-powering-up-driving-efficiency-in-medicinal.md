In early-stage drug discovery, library synthesis is a critical engine for driving Structure-Activity Relationship (SAR) exploration. However, traditional workflows are frequently bottlenecked by material waste, manual compound handling, and rigid IT infrastructure. In a recent SPT Labtech webinar , Matthias Schmid (Senior Scientist at Boehringer Ingelheim) and Cory Tiller (Product Manager at SPT Labtech) broke down how microscale synthesis and smart automated sample storage are radically transforming medicinal chemistry workflows.
