Cambridge, UK, and Madison, USA, 9 February 2026 : SPT Labtech, a global leader in the design and development of automated instrumentation and consumables for life sciences, and BellBrook Labs , a pioneer in high-throughput screening (HTS) assay tools, today announced a collaboration to advance cancer research. Together, the companies have developed a miniaturized, high-throughput screening platform for the discovery and profiling of Vacuolar protein sorting associated protein 4B (VPS4B) ATPase inhibitors, addressing a critical bottleneck in early-stage oncology drug discovery.
